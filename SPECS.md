# Specs

One entry per feature, written before the code. Ten lines is enough.

Template:

## <feature name> (<date>)

- Purpose: what it does and who calls it.
- Inputs: types and where they come from.
- Outputs: types and where they go.
- Failure cases: what can go wrong and what happens then.
- Tests: the cases that must pass, including the one that must fail.
- Out of scope: what this deliberately does not do.
